# Guardy

A Chrome extension that watches emails and websites for phishing, fake login pages, and messages that try to change banking information. It is designed for older adults: large type, plain English, and a calm “take your time” tone instead of panic.

Scanning runs on the device. Messages are not sent to a server.

## Load it in Chrome

1. Open `chrome://extensions`
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right)
3. Click **Load unpacked**
4. Choose this `guardy` folder
5. Pin the gold shield, then open **Settings** and add a family name, phone number, and bank
6. Click the shield → **Practice with a fake scam** to see the warnings without using real mail

It works on Gmail, Outlook on the web, Yahoo Mail, and ordinary websites. Press `Alt+G` to scan the current page.

## What Guardy watches for

- Rushed language and locked-account threats
- Requests to change bank accounts, routing numbers, or direct deposit
- Fake IRS, Social Security, Medicare, Apple, or Microsoft messages
- Links that say one website but go somewhere else
- The “grandparent emergency” money scam
